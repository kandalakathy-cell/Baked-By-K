Organisation Name: Baked by B

Baked by B was founded from a passion for baking and a love for creating desserts that bring people together. What started as baking for family and friends soon grew into a small home-based bakery after the quality, creativity, and taste of the baked treats received positive feedback. Today, Baked by B specialises in freshly baked cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and a variety of sweet desserts for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, and many other special occasions. Every order is carefully prepared using quality ingredients, with the aim of making every celebration a little sweeter and every customers feel valued

Mission Statement

The mission of Baked by B is to create fresh, high-quality baked goods that bring joy to every celebration. We are committed to using quality ingredients, providing excellent customer service, and creating beautifully crafted treats that make every customer's special occasion memorable.

Vision Statement

The vision of Baked by B is to become a trusted and well-known bakery recognised for creativity, quality, and exceptional customer service. We aim to grow our business while continuing to make every celebration sweeter with delicious and beautifully crafted baked goods.

Target Audience

Baked by B is designed for individuals, families, and businesses seeking beautifully crafted, high quality baked goods for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, corporate events, and other memorable occasions. The bakery welcomes customers of all ages who appreciate fresh, delicious desserts made with care.

Website Goals and Objectives

The main goal of the Baked by B website is to create a professional online presence where customers can discover the bakery, explore the wide range of freshly baked products, view special offers, learn about the services available and easily find contact information. The website will provide customers with a simple and enjoyable way to browse the bakery's products and make enquiries or place orders. The website will also help increase brand awareness, attract new customers, build trust, and strengthen relationships with existing customers. The success of the website will be measured by the number of website visitors, customer enquiries, orders received, and returning customers. An increase in these areas will indicate that the website is successfully supporting the growth of Baked by B.

Current Website Analysis

Baked by B currently operates without an official website. Customers place orders mainly through social media platforms and WhatsApp. While these platforms are useful for communication, they do not provide customers with one place to view products, learn about the bakery, or access important information. An official website will improve the business's online presence and make it easier for both new and existing customers to interact with the bakery.

Strengths

• Freshly baked, homemade products made with quality ingredients.

• Custom cakes and desserts for a variety of occasions.

• Friendly, reliable, and personalised customer service.

• Strong customer relationships through social media and WhatsApp.

Weaknesses

• No official website to showcase the bakery.

• Customers cannot easily browse the full product range.

• Limited online information about products, prices, and services.

• New customers may find it difficult to learn about the business.

Areas for Improvement

Developing an official website will provide customers with one convenient place to explore Baked by B. Visitors will be able to browse products, view cake designs, learn more about the bakery, discover special offers, place enquiries, and find contact information quickly and easily. The website will also strengthen the bakery's online presence, improve customer experience, and help attract new customers while supporting the continued growth of the business.

Proposed Website Features and Functionality

The Baked by B website will be designed to provide customers with an easy and enjoyable browsing experience. Each page will have a clear purpose, allowing visitors to quickly find the information they need and learn more about the bakery's products and services.

• Home

The Home page will welcome visitors to Baked by B and introduce the bakery. It will showcase featured baked goods, highlight the bakery's passion for quality, and encourage visitors to explore the rest of the website.

• About Us

The About Us page will share the story of Baked by B, including the bakery's history, mission, vision, and values. This page will help customers learn more about the business and build trust in the brand.

• Products

The Products page will display a selection of freshly baked cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and other desserts. Each product category will include images, descriptions, and starting prices to help customers choose the perfect treats for their special occasions.

• Special Offers

The Special Offers page will keep customers informed about current promotions, seasonal discounts, and limited time deals available at the bakery.

• Custom Orders

The Custom Orders page will explain how customers can request personalised cakes and desserts for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, and other celebrations. It will also provide information about the ordering process and customisation options.

• Contact Us

The Contact Us page will include the bakery's contact details, social media links, business hours, and a contact form where customers can send enquiries or request more information.

Website Functionality

The website will include a navigation menu on every page, making it easy for customers to move around the website. High-quality product images and detailed descriptions will help visitors explore the bakery's offerings, while clear contact information will make it simple to place orders or ask questions. The website will be designed to provide a user-friendly experience and encourage customers to return.

Design and User Experience

The Baked by B website will have a simple, clean, and easy-to-use design. Each page will have clear headings, readable text, and high-quality images to help customers learn more about the bakery and its products. The website will use a consistent layout on every page to make browsing simple and organised. A navigation menu will appear at the top of every page, allowing visitors to move easily between the Home, About Us, Products, Special Offers, Custom Orders, and Contact Us pages. Product images and descriptions will help customers explore the bakery's products, while the simple layout will ensure that information is easy to find and understand.

Technical Requirements

The Baked by B website will be developed using HTML in Visual Studio Code. The website will consist of multiple linked web pages, images, and a navigation menu to allow users to move easily between pages. If the business decides to publish the website online in the future, a domain name and web hosting service will be required. Timeline and Milestones

Part 3 – Baked by K Website

Introduction

For Part 3, I continued developing my home bakery website. I changed the name from Baked by B to Baked by K and updated the website to reflect the new name. I also improved the website by adding JavaScript features and making changes to the CSS. These improvements were made to make the website more interactive, user-friendly and responsive on different screen sizes.

CSS Improvements

I used CSS to maintain the website's design and keep the pages consistent. The website uses pink, white and brown colours, with Georgia as the main font. I used borders, rounded corners, shadows, hover effects and image animations to improve the appearance of the pages. I also added comments to explain different sections of the CSS code, making it easier to understand.

I added a media query to improve the website's layout on mobile phones and tablets. This adjusts the text, navigation links, images and forms to fit smaller screens better.

JavaScript Features

I added JavaScript features to make the website more interactive. A welcome message appears when visitors open the home page. The order form checks that customers enter a 10-digit phone number and do not select an event date in the past. I also added an order preparation reminder that suggests placing an order between five and seven days before the selected event date.

The product search allows visitors to search for products, while the weekly offers feature allows them to show or hide the offers. The contact form checks the phone number entered and prepares an email containing the customer's details. The email opens in the visitor's email application for them to review and send.

Google Maps

I added a Google Maps embed to the contact page to show the general Johannesburg area. This gives visitors an idea of the area where the bakery hopes to operate in the future.

Testing

I tested the website in the browser to check that the pages opened correctly and the JavaScript features worked. I checked the welcome message, order form validation, order preparation reminder, product search, weekly offers and contact form. I also used the mobile preview to check that the website layout fitted smaller screens.

Conclusion

The changes made in Part 3 improved the appearance and functionality of Baked by K. Changing the bakery's name, improving the CSS and adding JavaScript features helped make the website more interactive and easier to use. Testing also helped me check that the main features worked as expected.
